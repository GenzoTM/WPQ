2.0.0
- Paragraph breaks are kept (blank line between paragraphs); also $b.
- Quest title, progress window ("Continue/Cancel"), completion window, objective lines and the
  quest-log list are translated. Headers, buttons and zone names come from UIStrings.lua.
- Context aware: NPC window uses GetTitleText, quest log uses its own selection (fixes wrong quest picked).
- Untranslated quests are restored to the original Blizzard font/alignment/width.
- Latin words/numbers keep reading order inside Persian text; brackets and < > are mirrored; "5." punctuation fixed.
- Lam-alef ligatures, Arabic yeh/kaf normalised to Persian forms.
- Quest ID support, $n $c $r $g variables, scroll frames refreshed, apply() debounced.
- Errors are no longer silent: /wpq errors, /wpq debug, /wpq dump.
- tools/make_db.py builds Database.lua from translations.json.
